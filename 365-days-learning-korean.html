365 Days of Learning Korean breaks the Korean language down into small, manageable daily steps instead of overwhelming you with everything at once. Across 52 pages, it builds your vocabulary, grammar and confidence gradually, day by day.

Who This Book Is For

365 Days of Learning Korean is built for complete beginners who want a clear daily routine instead of jumping between random YouTube videos and apps. It suits K-drama fans, K-pop listeners, and anyone planning a trip to Korea who wants to pick up real, usable Korean.

FAQ

Do I need to already know Hangul to start?

No. The early days walk you through Hangul from scratch before moving on to vocabulary and simple grammar.

How much time does each day take?

Most days are designed to take 10-20 minutes, so it's manageable even on busy days.

Is this enough to become fluent?

It builds a strong beginner-to-intermediate foundation. Fluency takes ongoing practice, but this gives you a structured, consistent starting point.
