How This GPA Calculator Works

Each letter grade is converted to grade points on the standard 4.0 scale (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0.0), multiplied by the course's credit hours, then divided by your total credit hours. This student mark calculator gives you an instant, accurate semester GPA with no manual math needed.
