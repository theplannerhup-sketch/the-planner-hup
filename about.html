ABOUT US

The Story Behind The Planner Hup

A small studio for people who love to plan, learn and dream — one page at a time.

Who We Are

The Planner Hup started with a simple idea: planning should feel calm, not overwhelming. We design digital planners, stationery and study tools for students and everyday planners who want structure without it feeling like homework.

Every planner in our shop is designed in-house, page by page, with a soft pink-and-rose aesthetic that we hope makes opening your planner something you actually look forward to.

Beyond Planners

Alongside our planners, we also publish books and Urdu novels for readers who want to get lost in a good story. Our first novel, "بھیڑیے کی روح" (Bheriya ki Rooh), was written to bring atmospheric, original Urdu fiction to readers who love a haunting, well-told tale.
