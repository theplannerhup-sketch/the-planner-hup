Why Choose a Digital Planner for iPad?

Switching from paper to a digital planner on iPad means you always have your schedule with you, without carrying an extra notebook. Because our planners are undated, you can start on any day of the year and keep using the same file for months — nothing goes to waste if you skip a week or fall behind.

Every planner is built with clickable hyperlinked tabs, so instead of scrolling through dozens of pages to find today's date, you tap once and land exactly where you need to be. Combined with the Apple Pencil, GoodNotes turns your iPad into a notebook that feels natural to write in, while still giving you the speed and organization of a digital tool — searchable notes, unlimited copies of the same page, and zero risk of losing a physical planner.

For students juggling multiple classes, digital planners also make it easy to duplicate a weekly spread every time you need a fresh page, insert extra note pages next to your schedule, and keep everything in a single file instead of several notebooks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this planner work with GoodNotes 6?
Yes. All our digital planners are tested on both GoodNotes 5 and GoodNotes 6, and the hyperlinked tabs work the same way in both versions.

Can I use this on an Android tablet?
The PDF itself will open on Android note-taking apps like Xodo or Noteshelf, though hyperlinked navigation works most reliably in GoodNotes and Notability on iPad.

Do I need internet access after downloading?
No. Once the PDF is saved to your device and imported into your app, it works fully offline.

Can I change the colors or fonts?
The core layout is fixed, but you can highlight, write, and add stickers in your favorite colors directly inside GoodNotes using the built-in tools.

What if the hyperlinks stop working after import?
This usually means the PDF was imported as an image instead of a document — re-import using GoodNotes' "Import PDF" option rather than "Insert Image" to keep the tabs clickable.
