How Gifting Works

  1. Pick the planner, book, or novel you'd like to gift from our collection.
  2. Message us on WhatsApp and mention it's a gift — let us know the recipient's name if you'd like it personalized.
  3. Confirm payment with us, just like a regular order.
  4. We'll send the file straight to you (or directly to the recipient, if you prefer) so you can share the surprise your way.

Want It Personalized?

Let us know on WhatsApp if you'd like a short personal note added, or the recipient's name mentioned when we send the file — a small touch that makes the gift feel extra special.
