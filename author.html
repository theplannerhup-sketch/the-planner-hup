Founder, Designer & Author behind The Planner Hup

About Sania

Sania Intazar is the creative force behind The Planner Hup — from the very first line of code to every planner page and every story on this site. She designed and built this entire website herself, hand-designed every planner in the collection, and writes both non-fiction books and fiction novels published under her own name.

Designer of Every Planner

Every planner on this site — from the layouts to the colors to the little details that make planning feel special — is designed by Sania herself. Nothing here is a template; each planner is built with real students and planning-lovers in mind, based on what actually helps people stay organized.

Her Books

Beyond planners, Sania has authored several books available on The Planner Hup, including 365 Days of Learning Korean, Glow-Up Korean Secrets, Book Reading Journal, Huqooq Allah Aur Huqooq-ul-Ibad, and Hazrat Ali Ka Majmua-e-Quran.

Her Writing Journey

Sania started The Planner Hup with a simple idea — to create beautiful tools that help people plan, learn, and dream. Over time, that same passion for helping readers grow found its way into fiction, giving birth to stories that explore identity, emotion, and the quiet moments that shape a life. Every novel she writes carries that same intention: to make readers feel something real.
