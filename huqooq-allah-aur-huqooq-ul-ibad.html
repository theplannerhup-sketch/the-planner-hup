About This Urdu Book

Huqooq Allah Aur Huqooq-ul-Ibad is an Urdu book exploring two of the most important foundations in Islam: our rights and responsibilities toward Allah, and our rights and responsibilities toward one another as people.

Written in simple, accessible Urdu, it's meant for readers who want a clear, practical understanding of these concepts in everyday life — not just as abstract ideas, but as things that shape how we treat others and worship with sincerity.

This book is well suited for personal reading, reflection, or sharing with family.

Who This Book Is For

This book is for readers who want a clear, practical understanding of the two pillars of Islamic responsibility: our rights toward Allah and our rights toward other people. It's written for a general Urdu-speaking audience, not specifically for scholars or students of Islamic law.

It's especially useful for anyone looking for straightforward guidance on how these two areas of responsibility show up in everyday life.

FAQ

What does Huqooq Allah and Huqooq-ul-Ibad mean?

Huqooq Allah refers to our obligations toward Allah, while Huqooq-ul-Ibad refers to our obligations toward other people. The book explains both in practical, everyday terms.

Is this book suitable for beginners?

Yes, it's written in plain Urdu and doesn't require prior background in Islamic studies.

Does it include practical, everyday examples?

Yes, the concepts are explained with everyday situations in mind, rather than staying purely theoretical.
